About

Engineering AI that survives contact with reality.

I'm a B.Tech student specializing in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Data Science, and I spend most of my time turning research ideas into things that actually run in production — not just notebooks that work once.

My work sits at the intersection of deep learning, computer vision, and NLP. I've built a masked language model from scratch to understand how transformers really learn, trained LSTMs for sequence prediction, and wired computer vision into a ROS2 robot so it could see and react to the world around it.

I care as much about the engineering as I do about the modeling — serving models behind FastAPI, securing endpoints with JWT, structuring clean data pipelines, and thinking about how a system behaves under real users, not just on a benchmark. I'm currently deepening my work in generative AI and agentic systems.
